Rita AI- Privacy Policy
In compliance with the provisions of the applicable regulations on personal-data protection, we inform you that the information processed through the Rita AI mobile application (“the App”) will be handled by Dilek İrem Özbay as the Data Controller.

1. What data we collect
Rita AI is designed for anonymous use. We do not request or store any information that directly identifies you—such as name, e-mail address, phone number, or official ID.
The App only processes limited data categories necessary for its operation:
●	Account Information: anonymous username created by the user when opening an account.
●	Content Information: the text inputs you provide while using the App (messages sent to the AI, chat histories, dates, summaries, habit logs, statistics and feedback).
●	Operational Security Information: device identifier and technical data used to access the App.
●	Optional Profile Information: non-mandatory information such as age, gender, height, weight, and fitness-related goals (weight loss, weight gain, or maintenance) provided by the User to enable personalized habit tracking and recommendations.

2. How we use information
The data described above may be processed for the following purposes:
●	To create and manage your user account.
●	To provide the core functions of the App (text-based chat experience and AI-generated responses).
●	To ensure App security, maintenance, and technical improvement.
●	To provide user support and track or respond to user notifications.
●	To perform storage and archiving activities.
●	To carry out advertising or promotional activities, only where you have provided consent.
●	To follow up legal or administrative obligations where required.
Data may be used for in-app advertising or promotional purposes only where explicit consent has been provided by the user.
3. How we collect the information
Data is collected automatically when you use the App through secure digital means.
You may also choose to provide information voluntarily—for example, by submitting feedback.
Rita AI does not collect information from third-party sources and does not use tracking cookies.

4. Who processes and stores the data
For the correct operation of the App, Rita AI relies on secure third-party service providers acting as data processors:
●	Gemini:
The AI-powered chat feature of the Application is provided through Google Cloud Vertex AI and Google's Gemini models. In this context, user messages are transmitted to Google's systems solely for the purpose of generating AI responses and delivering the requested service.
Data processed through Vertex AI is not used to train or improve Google's foundation models. Google provides contractual and compliance commitments regarding data protection and security through its cloud services and internationally recognized information security standards and certifications. User data is processed only to provide the AI functionality requested by the user and in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.
●	Google Firebase:
Data security, error analysis operations of the Application are supported by Google Firebase.
●	Supabase:
Authentication and login processes are handled via Supabase hosted on a self-managed server. User authentication data is processed within this infrastructure and is not transferred to third-party systems outside the designated server environment.
●	Local Server:Chat history, habit logs, and usage statistics are stored on a locally hosted server under the Application’s control. This data is processed and retained within the designated server environment and is not transferred to external third-party systems.
5. Data retention

Your information is stored only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined above and to maintain service quality.
After that, it is deleted or permanently anonymised through secure technical measures.
The device identifier, username, chat and habit logs used to access the application are retained for a period of six months.
Chat history is stored in anonymized form for five years for reference in statistical analysis and user support processes.

6. Disclosure of information
All information is treated as confidential.

Data may be disclosed only to:
●	public authorities when legally required; or
●	the technical providers mentioned above (see article 4), strictly for service delivery.

7. Cookies and similar technologies
Rita AI does not use cookies, trackers, or similar technologies.

8. Your rights
Depending on your place of residence, you may exercise some or all of the following rights in relation to your personal data:
User Rights
You have the right to access your personal data, request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data, request deletion of your data, and request restriction of certain processing activities where applicable.
You may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority in your country.
Where applicable, you may request a copy of your data in a portable format.
You can exercise your rights by contacting destek@ritaai.app
For security purposes, we may require limited verification to confirm that the request is made by the account owner. This verification may be performed through the in-app email support channel. We do not request identity documents or additional sensitive personal information for verification purposes.
9. Security measures
We adopt technical and organisational measures to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all information processed in the App.

10. Updates to this policy
Rita AI may modify this Privacy Policy from time to time.
The latest version will always be available within the App and will indicate the “Effective Date.”
The Effective Date of the current version of this policy is 24th June 2026. 
Significant changes will be communicated to users in advance.
